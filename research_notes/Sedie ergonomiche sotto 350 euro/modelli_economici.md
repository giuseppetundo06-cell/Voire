# Budget ergonomic office chairs (about 120–230 €) available in Italy: specs and review verdicts

Research date: 21 Nov 2026. User context: Italy, chronic low back pain, 2–3+ h/day of sitting after standing all day at work, ideal budget about 200 €.

**Method and limits (important for the report writer):** Every page fetch failed in this session. rtings.com, btod.com and remotechance.com did not resolve because they are outside the session's allowed network domains. All findings below therefore come from search-engine results and snippets, not from reading the full pages. Each figure is attributed to the page the search tool cited. I could not open the Amazon.it pages to confirm a live price, so prices are "as reported by the cited page", with that page's own date where one was given. Check prices again before buying, especially around Black Friday (late Nov 2026).

## 1. Sihoo models (M18, M57, Doro C300, Doro S100, M90/M93/M102)

### Takeaway
In the €120–230 band, Sihoo's realistic options are the **M18** (about 160–180 €, foam seat, 2-way adjustable lumbar, no seat-depth slider) and the **M57** (mesh, 2D lumbar for height and depth, no seat-depth slider). The base **Doro C300** has better dynamic lumbar support, but it sits at about 270 € list price on Sihoo EU, above the target, and it also has no seat-depth adjustment. Only the C300 Pro and the Doro S100 add a seat slider. I found no "M90" and no "Doro S120". "M102" is only a colour/SKU suffix.

- The Doro S100 has the best spec sheet (seat slider, 4D arms, dual dynamic lumbar), but I found no EU/Italian price, so whether it fits the band is unknown.

## 2. IKEA models (Markus, Järvfjället, Flintan, Hattefjäll, Alefjäll, Millberget)

### Takeaway
Of the IKEA models, only the **Järvfjället** has both **adjustable seat depth and adjustable-height lumbar**, at about 199 € in Italy (older source). That makes it IKEA's best fit for this user. It earned "befriedigend (3.2)" from Stiftung Warentest in 2024, finishing second. The Markus has fixed seat depth and a lumbar that reviewers dislike. The Flintan has no seat-depth, backrest or tilt adjustment. The Hattefjäll was rated "gut" by Stiftung Warentest in 2021 but costs about 249–299 €.

### Cited findings
- **Which? (UK)** says the Järvfjället has adjustable backrest, seat height and **seat depth**, but non-adjustable seat tilt. The **Markus and Flintan have no seat-depth adjustment**. The Flintan's backrest, seat depth and tilt cannot be adjusted — [Which?](https://www.which.co.uk/reviews/office-chairs/article/best-office-chairs-and-buying-guide-aNpAi8A3zmJM)
- **BTOD (hands-on)**: Markus seat depth is fixed at 18.5". The Järvfjället seat slides over roughly 17–19". The Järvfjället wins on lumbar and seat depth, while the Markus wins on recline and seat comfort. The author "strongly dislikes" the Markus lumbar — [BTOD](https://www.btod.com/blog/ikea-markus-vs-jarvfjallet/) (snippet only; site blocked for fetch)
- Some reviewers find the Järvfjället seat hard and its lumbar "presses too much", especially for lighter users — [thefurnishedreview.com / osteoxfit.com, via search](https://thefurnishedreview.com/reviews/ikea-markus-vs-jarvfjallet); [osteoxfit.com](https://osteoxfit.com/articles/ikea-jarvfjallet-review)
- **Stiftung Warentest 02/2024**: only 1 of 9 chairs was rated "gut" (Höffner Home Worx Office 95, about 400 €). The IKEA Järvfjället came 2nd with "befriedigend (3.2)" — [t-online](https://www.t-online.de/ratgeber/haushalt-und-wohnen/einrichtung/id_90681920/buerostuehle-im-test-nur-ein-neuer-stuhl-ueberzeugt-die-stiftung-warentest.html); [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.com/Buerostuehle-im-Warentest-Nur-einer-im-Test-ist-gut-der-Rest-ueberraschend-schlecht.796122.0.html)
- **Stiftung Warentest 2021**: the IKEA Hattefjäll was rated "gut" (one of five "gut" chairs) — [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/karriere/buerostuehle-im-test-die-besten-buerostuehle-fuers-homeoffice-nur-fuenf-modelle-mit-gut-bewertet/27562414.html)
- Tom's Guide ran an "I tried every IKEA office chair — here are the 3 models I'd actually buy" piece. A search summary says it describes the Hattefjäll as having limited adjustment (seat height and armrests). I could not read the article to confirm which 3 chairs it picked — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/home/home-office/i-tried-every-ikea-office-chair-here-are-the-3-models-id-actually-buy)
- **Italian prices (Designmag, about 2 years old)**: Järvfjället with arms about 199 €, Hattefjäll with arms about 299 €, Alefjäll black about 269 €, Flintan about 69 € — [designmag.it](https://www.designmag.it/articolo/sedie-da-ufficio-ikea/62306/)
- **Recent prices in other markets**: IKEA Switzerland lists the Flintan at 119.95 CHF, Millberget 99.95 CHF, Hattefjäll 249 CHF, Alefjäll 349 CHF — [IKEA CH](https://www.ikea.com/ch/it/cat/sedie-da-scrivania-20652/f/sedie-da-scrivania-con-rotelle-f-feature--53032/). IKEA Sweden lists the Järvfjället at 3,495 SEK and the Alefjäll at 3,795 SEK — [IKEA SE](https://www.ikea.com/se/sv/cat/skrivbords-kontorsstolar-20652/)
- The Järvfjället listing includes adjustable lumbar, armrests and headrest — [IKEA UK](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/cat/desk-chairs-20652/). The Millberget is a swivel chair with height adjustment and armrests — [IKEA SE](https://www.ikea.com/se/sv/cat/skrivbords-kontorsstolar-20652/)
- Järvfjället US price conflict: $299 vs $399.99 depending on the source — [btod.com / interiorinsider.com, via search](https://www.interiorinsider.com/article/ikea-markus-vs-jarvfjallet)

### Inferences
- The Järvfjället is IKEA's only ergonomically complete option near 200 €: seat depth plus height-adjustable lumbar. It also has independent lab validation (Stiftung Warentest), even if only "befriedigend". In Italy it can be tried in store before buying, which is a real advantage for someone with back pain.
- The Markus (fixed depth, lumbar widely criticised), Flintan and Millberget are weak choices for chronic low back pain. The Hattefjäll and Alefjäll are above the ~200 € target.

### Gaps
- No current ikea.com/it prices (2026) could be confirmed. Designmag's prices are about 2 years old.
- Alefjäll and Millberget seat depth and lumbar specs were not found. I could not confirm whether the Hattefjäll has adjustable lumbar.
- IKEA warranty terms (IKEA generally advertises a 10-year warranty on some office chairs) and EN 1335 claims were not verified in this session.
